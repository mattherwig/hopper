How it works

Browser rules for apps, one list for everything else

Nothing runs in the background. Hopper reads what macOS and your apps already know, when you ask.

Agents

  • Status straight from the agentsNo hooks to install, nothing to set up.
  • Done means unseenFinished, and you haven't looked yet.
  • Lands in the exact placeRight to the split, pane, or session.

Search

  • Fuzzy finds everythingTypos and partial words still match.
  • Bookmarks and Recently ClosedBack to the open tab, or reopened.
  • Private stays privateIncognito windows never show up.

Apps

  • Back works like a browserSwitch any other way to reset Forward.
  • Picks up where you left offBack to the window you were in.
  • Your trail, your rulesRemove or Exclude apps from History.

Setup

  1. Install Hopper from the Raycast Store.
  2. Record hotkeys in Raycast Settings → Extensions → Hopper (extensions can't set them). Recommended: the ones shown above.
  3. First time in Search: allow Automation and Accessibility when asked.
