How it works

Browser rules for apps, one list for everything else

Nothing runs in the background. Hopper reads what macOS and your apps already know, when you ask.

Agents

  • Status straight from the agentsNo hooks to install, nothing to set up.
  • Done means unseenFinished since you last looked, in its app or from Hopper.
  • Lands in the exact placeThe terminal split, herdr pane, or Claude or Cursor session.

Search

  • Fuzzy finds everythingPartial words and typos still land on the right tab.
  • Recently ClosedReopen tabs, pages, and notes from the past week.
  • Private stays privateIncognito and private windows never show up.

Apps

  • Back works like a browserSwitch any other way and forward history resets.
  • Picks up where you left offApps come back to the window you were in.
  • Your trail, your rulesRemove or Exclude apps you don't want in History.

Setup

  1. Install Hopper from the Raycast Store.
  2. Record hotkeys in Raycast Settings → Extensions → Hopper (extensions can't set them). Recommended: the ones shown above.
  3. First time in Search: allow Automation and Accessibility when asked.
