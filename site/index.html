How it works

Browser rules for apps, one list for everything else

Nothing runs in the background. Hopper reads what macOS and your apps already know, when you ask.

Agents

  • Status comes from the agents themselves. No hooks to install.
  • Done means finished since you last looked.
  • Jumps land in the exact place: the terminal split, herdr pane, or Claude or Cursor session.

Search

  • Forgives typos: "caude" finds Claude.
  • Recently Closed reopens tabs, pages, and notes from the past week.
  • Private windows never show up.

Apps

  • Back works like a browser: switch any other way and forward history resets.
  • You land in the window you left.
  • Remove or Exclude apps you don't want in History.

Claude chats and Muse are read from the app's on-screen sidebar Notion and Obsidian from their tab bars, so an app update can change what Hopper finds; if nothing is found, the app's windows are listed instead.

Setup

  1. Install Hopper from the Raycast Store.
  2. Record hotkeys in Raycast Settings → Extensions → Hopper (extensions can't set them). Recommended: the ones shown above.
  3. First time in Search: allow Automation and Accessibility when asked.
