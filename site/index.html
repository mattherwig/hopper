Jump to any app, tab, or agent.

Step back through recent apps like browser history, search every tab and session in one list, and jump to the AI agent that needs you. Free for Raycast.

Three ways to jump

Apps, search, and agents

Move between apps with hotkeys, like Back and Forward in a browser. Open one list of everything open inside them and pick what you want. Or go straight to the AI agent that's waiting on you, wherever it runs.

Apps

Browser-style history for the apps you've been using.

  • Back / Forward⇧⌘[ ⇧⌘]Step back to the app you used before this one, and keep going. Forward retraces.
  • Toggle⌘ ⌘Flip between your two most recent apps. Run again to flip back.
  • HistoryRunning apps from most to least recent. Jump to any of them, or remove and exclude apps.

Search

One search across the tabs, windows, sessions, and agents of every running app.

  • Search⌃⌘Search browser tabs, terminal tabs, herdr workspaces, Claude and Muse sessions, Notion tabs, any app's windows, and agents, then jump straight to one.
  • Search Current AppThe same, for just the app you're in.

Agents

Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, herdr, and other agents, wherever they run, by what they need from you.

  • AgentsEvery running agent grouped by Needs You, Done, Working, and Idle, filterable by project. Jump to its terminal pane, herdr pane, or session in the Claude app or Cursor.
  • Next Agent⌃⌥AJump to the agent that has waited longest for you: needs input or approval first, then finished and unseen. Run again for the next.

How it works

Browser rules for apps, one list for everything else

Apps come from the order macOS already keeps for Cmd+Tab, so nothing runs in the background. Search reads each app when you open it.

Apps

  • The first Back takes a snapshot of your app order and moves one step back. Further Backs go deeper; Forward retraces.
  • Switching any other way starts fresh. Click an app or Cmd+Tab and the forward history is dropped, just like visiting a new page in a browser.
  • Remove from History (⌃X) hides an app until you use it again; Exclude (⌃⇧X) hides it for good.

Search

  • Apps are ordered by recent use; within an app, active tabs come first.
  • Search forgives typos ("caude" finds Claude) and matches app names first: an app's own tabs before tabs that mention it.
  • Picking an entry selects it in its app and brings the app to the front.
  • Copy URL and Copy Title are in the action panel.
  • Recently Closed reopens browser tabs, Notion pages, and documents closed since Search last looked (a week, up to 100). Never terminals, chats, or private windows.
  • Apps Jumper can't read show under Unavailable, with a shortcut to the settings pane that fixes it.

Agents

  • Status comes from the agents themselves: Claude Code's list of running sessions, the Codex daemon, Cursor's flags, herdr's detection. No hooks to install.
  • Done means finished since you last looked, in the agent's own app or by jumping to it from Jumper.
  • Jumping goes to where it runs: the exact iTerm split or cmux terminal, the herdr pane, the Claude session, the Cursor window.
  • Projects are git repositories; worktrees count as their main repository.

Setup

  1. Install Jumper from the Raycast Store.
  2. Assign hotkeys to Back, Forward, Toggle, Search (⌃⌘), and Next Agent (⌃⌥A) in Raycast Settings → Extensions → Jumper.
  3. First time in Search: allow Automation and Accessibility when asked.
